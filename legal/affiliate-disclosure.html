Commercial Transparency & Affiliate Disclosure

Effective Date: September 7, 2026 • Version: 2.6.0 • Compliance: US FTC (16 CFR Part 255), UK Advertising Standards Authority (CAP Code), EU Directive 2005/29/EC

1. The Foundation of Our Independence

Helmetsan (“we,” “our,” or “the Platform”), operated by Ash Digital Services / Ashish Digital Services, is an independent motorcycle helmet discovery engine, technical comparative database, and safety research portal. Our fundamental mission is to provide motorcycle riders worldwide with uncompromised, technically rigorous, and mathematically grounded helmet evaluations.

Operating a high-performance, real-time database infrastructure—including global multi-currency tracking, volumetric CAD modeling analysis, multilingual translation engines, and safety matrix computations—requires substantial computational and human resources. To sustain our operations and remain free for riders globally, Helmetsan monetizes through affiliate marketing partnerships and programmatic display advertising.

In full compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255), the United Kingdom Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) CAP Code, and European Union consumer transparency laws, we provide this comprehensive disclosure detailing how our financial partnerships work.

2. Plain English Summary for Riders

  • When you click certain links on Helmetsan directing to authorized online motorcycle gear retailers and subsequently complete a purchase, Helmetsan may earn a small referral commission from the merchant.
  • You pay exactly the same price: Using an affiliate link never increases the retail purchase price, shipping fee, or applicable taxes you pay. In many instances, our automated coupon tracking may actually reduce your final checkout cost.
  • Editorial Independence Is Absolute: Our safety scores, noise ratings, weight evaluations, and editorial recommendations are calculated independently of whether a helmet brand or retailer offers an affiliate program or pays a higher commission percentage.

3. Authorized Affiliate Partner Networks

Helmetsan maintains direct and programmatic affiliate marketing relationships with verified, authorized motorcycle gear distributors, factory dealers, and major international e-commerce platforms. These include, but are not limited to:

  • Amazon Services LLC Associates Program: Helmetsan is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com, Amazon.co.uk, Amazon.de, Amazon.fr, Amazon.es, Amazon.it, Amazon.ca, and affiliated regional domains.
  • Comoto Holdings (RevZilla, Cycle Gear, J&P Cycles): Leading North American motorcycle apparel and helmet retailers.
  • European Specialists: FC-Moto GmbH (Germany), Motoin GmbH (Germany), Chromeburner Motorgear (Netherlands), Champion Helmets (Netherlands), and Louis Moto.
  • Affiliate Networks & Tracking Providers: Impact.com, CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction), ShareASale, Rakuten Marketing, Awin, and Sovrn Commerce.

When you click an outbound merchant button (e.g., “Check Price on RevZilla,” “View on FC-Moto,” “Buy on Amazon”), a unique tracking identifier is appended to the destination URL. This tracking cookie records that the referral originated from Helmetsan for a specified attribution window (typically 24 hours to 30 days) and enables the merchant to credit our platform upon a verified sale.

4. How We Safeguard Editorial Integrity & Neutrality

Commercial relationships can easily create conflicts of interest if not strictly guarded. Helmetsan enforces the following organizational and technical safeguards:

  1. Separation of Engineering & Commerce: Our data ingestion team and algorithmic scoring pipelines operate autonomously. Product scores (including Impact Attenuation, Acoustic Noise Index, Ventilation Efficiency, and Optical Clarity) are calculated deterministically from verified laboratory test figures and engineering specifications.
  2. We Recommend Non-Affiliate Gear: If an independent boutique helmet (e.g., bespoke carbon racing helmets or specialized niche manufacturers) delivers superior safety performance for a specific riding discipline, we index, review, and recommend it with equal prominence, even if the manufacturer offers zero affiliate compensation.
  3. No Paid Reviews or Guaranteed Placements: Helmetsan does not accept financial compensation, free sample incentives, or corporate sponsorships in exchange for positive product reviews, higher placement in comparison matrices, or artificially inflated safety scores. Any sponsored editorial content, if ever published, will be clearly demarcated with prominent “SPONSORED ADVERTISEMENT” badges.
  4. Manufacturer Parity: Helmets from Japanese leaders (Shoei, Arai), European technical pioneers (Schuberth, AGV, Shark, Nolan, Scorpion), and American innovations (Bell, Klim, 6D) are evaluated under identical mathematical benchmark criteria.

5. Pricing, Currency Conversion & Merchant Inventory Disclaimers

Helmetsan provides dynamic price monitoring across international markets. However, the online retail landscape is volatile, and users must acknowledge the following technical realities:

  • Real-Time Discrepancies: While our automated data engines refresh merchant pricing and inventory feeds regularly, merchant inventory, promotional flash sales, coupon expirations, and stock levels change in real time. The definitive, legally binding price is always the checkout price displayed on the destination retailer’s website at the moment of purchase.
  • Geo-Pricing & Currency Fluctuations: Helmetsan utilizes automated geolocation to convert pricing into local currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, INR). Foreign exchange conversion rates are estimated based on public interbank exchange rates and may vary slightly from your credit card issuing bank’s foreign transaction fees.
  • Regional Certification & Import Legality: An international retailer (e.g., European e-commerce selling to a US resident) may supply a helmet certified solely to ECE 22.06 standards without DOT FMVSS 218 labeling. Helmetsan explicitly alerts riders to regional compliance, but you are solely responsible for ensuring any imported equipment is street-legal in your state, province, or country.

6. Programmatic Display Advertising (Google AdSense)

In addition to affiliate links, Helmetsan displays automated contextual advertising provided by Google AdSense and authorized programmatic demand partners. These advertisements are dynamically selected by automated ad networks based on page context, browser cookies, and your aggregated interests.

Display ads are distinctly styled, separated from editorial copy, and clearly labeled with indicators such as “Ad,” “Advertisement,” or the “Google AdChoices” logo. Helmetsan does not endorse the specific third-party products, services, or claims displayed within programmatic banner advertisements.

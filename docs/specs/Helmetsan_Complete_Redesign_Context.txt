HELMETSAN — COMPLETE UI/UX REDESIGN CONTEXT

Purpose

This document is the complete working context for redesigning and
improving Helmetsan.com using Antigravity IDE and Gemini 3.6. It is
intended to be given to the coding/AI agent so it understands the
product vision, current UI, problems identified, approved directions,
and implementation priorities.

Website: https://helmetsan.com/

Product positioning: GLOBAL HELMET INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

Helmetsan should NOT become a generic ecommerce helmet store. It should
be a structured helmet intelligence, discovery, comparison, safety,
fitment, accessory and price-information platform.

Core product principle: HELP THE RIDER MAKE A BETTER HELMET DECISION.

The site should answer: 1. What helmet is this? 2. Is it safe? 3. Is it
suitable for me? 4. How does it compare? 5. What accessories fit it? 6.
Where can I buy it? 7. What evidence/sources support the information?

IMPORTANT DEVELOPMENT PRINCIPLES

-   This is an information architecture and UX redesign, not merely a
    cosmetic redesign.
-   Inspect the existing database/data model before making major
    structural changes.
-   Reuse actual data wherever possible.
-   NEVER fabricate product specifications, certifications,
    compatibility, prices, ratings, reviews, head shapes, weights, model
    dates, retailer availability, or statistics.
-   If information is unavailable, show an explicit
    unavailable/unverified state or omit the field.
-   Do not infer helmet compatibility simply because two products share
    a brand or appear visually related.
-   Do not invent AI scores unless a documented scoring methodology
    exists.
-   Data trustworthiness is more important than visual richness.
-   Fix data/image pipeline issues before hiding them with UI.
-   Avoid excessive card-based dashboard design.
-   Use a premium technical/editorial/data-driven aesthetic.
-   Primary colors: black, white/off-white, neutral gray, restrained
    Helmetsan red.
-   Semantic colors: green = verified/compatible, amber = uncertain,
    gray = unavailable.
-   Avoid excessive gradients, purple badges, heavy shadows, excessive
    red borders, fake scores, fake ratings and unnecessary animations.

============================================================ 1. HOMEPAGE
— APPROVED DIRECTION
============================================================

Current homepage concept: - Global Helmet Intelligence Platform - Hero:
“Find the Right Helmet. Compare Specs. Stay Compliant.” - Featured
helmet - Stats - Featured helmets - Brands - Accessories - Safety &
Certifications - Footer

Problems identified: - Homepage had too many disconnected catalog
sections. - Product discovery was not sufficiently decision-oriented. -
The site looked more like a database/ecommerce directory than an
intelligence platform. - Need stronger relationships between helmets,
brands, accessories, safety standards and comparison.

Homepage should ultimately communicate: “Find the right helmet,
understand why it is right, verify its safety, compare alternatives, and
find where to buy.”

Core homepage pathways: - Find a helmet - Compare helmets - Explore
brands - Understand safety standards - Find compatible accessories -
Browse by riding style/use case

Do not make the homepage too card-heavy.

============================================================ 38. GEMINI
/ ANTIGRAVITY INSTRUCTION STYLE
============================================================

When using Gemini through Antigravity:

-   Give it one major page or subsystem at a time.
-   Ask it to inspect existing code/data before editing.
-   Tell it explicitly which architecture is approved.
-   Tell it not to fabricate missing values.
-   Ask it to preserve existing routes and working functionality unless
    intentionally changed.
-   Ask it to report data/schema problems separately from UI problems.
-   Make it test desktop and mobile.
-   Make it inspect console/network errors for missing images.
-   Do not let it “solve” missing data by generating fake placeholders
    that look real.
-   Prefer deterministic components and reusable design-system
    primitives.

Suggested workflow: 1. Inspect. 2. Identify existing components/data
sources. 3. Plan. 4. Implement one section. 5. Test. 6. Fix data issues.
7. Continue.

============================================================ 40. SUCCESS
CRITERIA ============================================================

After redesign, a user should understand the following within
approximately the first screen:

What helmet? What type? What certification? Who is it for? What is the
quick verdict? What does it cost? Where can I buy it?

Within the next sections:

Is it safe? Will it fit? What are the important specs? How does it
compare? What accessories fit? What do riders say? Where does the
information come from?

The page should feel: - Trustworthy - Technical - Premium - Easy to
scan - Decision-oriented - Data-driven - Not overly commercial

FINAL PRINCIPLE

The helmet detail page is the center of Helmetsan.

Do not optimize for “more information visible.”

Optimize for:

“THE RIGHT INFORMATION AT THE RIGHT MOMENT, WITH CLEAR EVIDENCE.”

Helmetsan should not merely tell riders what a helmet is.

It should help them decide whether it is the right helmet for them.
